    WHY THIS BOOK

    Written to be worked through, not skimmed.

    ⚙

    Grounded in real systems

    Every chapter opens on a concrete scenario before it defines anything. You learn not just how a tool works, but where it belongs — and when reaching for it is the wrong call.

    📚

    One path, in order

    Sixty-three chapters across eight parts, numbered and sequential. You always know where you are and what comes next, instead of assembling a curriculum out of disconnected tutorials.

    🚀

    Written for the current stack

    An entire part is given to ethical hacking and testing. The skills that separate the next decade from the last, with hands-on labs you can actually run.

    WHAT YOU BUILD

    Real skills, not diagrams.

    Every part ends in something executable — a config, a script, a lab you can run.

    ~/cyber-knights
